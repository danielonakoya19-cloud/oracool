Good to know

A few answers,
before you ask.

How does hands-free voice work?

Open the console, tap Start conversation and allow microphone access. OraCool listens again after a spoken reply. Recognition depends on your browser; supported browsers can use a recording/transcription fallback. Voice listening pauses when the page is hidden or your device locks.

Do I need to verify my email to sign up?

New accounts use email and password without a signup email-code gate. Connecting a mailbox or verifying an email recipient is a separate permission step.

Is my conversation history saved?

Signed-in chats are saved on the server and mirrored to the operator’s Supabase database when configured. The browser also caches recent messages. AI providers may process submitted content under their own terms. Read the privacy policy before sharing sensitive information.

Can OraCool send messages?

Confirmed email is available to Enterprise users and admins once the operator configures the email provider (SendGrid). Recipients must be verified and consent to contact, and you review the destination and exact message before sending. Telephone calling and SMS are not available on OraCool.

Are paid plans trials or automatic renewals?

There is no free paid-plan trial. Plans currently grant access for 30 days after a successful payment. Review your account and checkout details for the payment method and access period before paying.

Does OraCool provide financial guarantees?

No. Market content is informational and educational. Trading tools are paper-only; outputs are not guarantees of profit or personalized professional advice.
